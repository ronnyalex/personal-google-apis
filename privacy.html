Privacy policy

personal-google-apis is a private app used only by its owner, Ronny Alex, with his own Google account.

  • Gmail and Google Calendar data is accessed only on the owner's own Mac, for the owner's own use.
  • No data is sold, shared with or transferred to third parties.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on that Mac and can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
